# Meeting preparation

Deliver a brief that helps participants achieve the meeting's purpose.

1. Establish the meeting's objective, audience, timing, and available context. If the objective is inferable, proceed with a labeled assumption; do not invent attendees or accepted roles.
2. Use [search](../../search/SKILL.md) for missing background within scope. Use [catchup](../../catchup/SKILL.md) if changes since a prior update matter, and [followups](../../followups/SKILL.md) when outstanding commitments affect the agenda. Reuse the evidence those skills gather.
3. Use [meeting](../../meeting/SKILL.md) in preparation mode to produce the relevant background, discussion questions, proposed agenda, and linked prereading. Keep recommendations distinguishable from recorded decisions.
4. Verify consequential dates, decisions, and action status, then finish through [write](../../write/SKILL.md).

Done when the user can enter the meeting knowing its purpose, the material facts, and the questions to resolve. Preparation does not include scheduling or contacting participants unless requested.
