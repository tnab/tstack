# Meeting notes

Deliver a faithful record of what the meeting evidence supports.

1. Use [meeting](../../meeting/SKILL.md) in notes mode with the transcript or contemporaneous notes. If only an agenda exists, request the meeting record or provide a labeled template.
2. Preserve decisions, discussion, objections, conditions, and commitments. Outside research can clarify background when permitted; it cannot establish what was said in the meeting.
3. If the user also asks to reconcile an action register or later updates, use [followups](../../followups/SKILL.md). Keep the original meeting record distinct from later status changes. Prepare proposed updates unless writing the register is authorized.
4. Check key attributions and actions against source passages, then finish through [write](../../write/SKILL.md).

Done when the notes distinguish recorded agreement from suggestions and identify unknown owners or dates without inventing them. Do not send a recap merely because it is ready.
