# Decision

Deliver a reasoned recommendation or faithful decision record.

1. Use [decide](../../decide/SKILL.md) to establish the decision, constraints, credible options, evidence, and decision authority. Distinguish advice from a decision already made.
2. Fill decision-changing evidence gaps through [search](../../search/SKILL.md) when allowed. Compare materially different options without invented scores or forced agreement. If none meets the constraints, say so.
3. For a consequential recommendation, use [review](../../review/SKILL.md) to challenge the reasoning and calculations. Resolve supported findings; preserve tradeoffs and conditions that could change the choice.
4. Finish through [write](../../write/SKILL.md), including the decisive reason and useful revisit conditions.

Done when the choice or remaining decision is clear, supported, and attributed correctly. A recommendation is not approval to execute it.
