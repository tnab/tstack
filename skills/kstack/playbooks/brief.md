# Brief

Deliver a concise synthesis of the in-scope record.

1. Give [brief](../../brief/SKILL.md) the audience, question, corpus, and any historical cutoff. Inventory the packet before claiming full coverage.
2. Keep disagreements, source authority, and missing material visible where they affect the answer. Use [search](../../search/SKILL.md) only for a material gap within the user's permitted scope.
3. Check the conclusion against supporting and conflicting sources, then finish through [write](../../write/SKILL.md).

Done when the reader can understand the supported answer, its implications, and any material coverage limits without reading a recap of every document.
