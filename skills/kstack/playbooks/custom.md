# Custom workflow

Build a proportionate workflow for a knowledge-work outcome that spans playbooks or needs a different sequence.

1. State the deliverable, source boundaries, available capabilities, and observable completion check. Resolve the riskiest uncertainty first. Keep a brief plan for work substantial enough to benefit from one.
2. Compose existing playbooks and specialist skills around the deliverables. Reuse evidence, give each parallel worker a bounded output, and order dependent steps. Perform an unfamiliar step directly when the available tools support it; do not invent a skill or claim a missing capability exists.
3. Complete and verify one useful unit at a time. Revise the approach when the evidence contradicts its premise. Keep a compact decision trail when the work is long or will be handed off; use the user's chosen destination and omit private content from public artifacts.
4. Inspect the combined result against the completion check and source record. Finish through [write](../../write/SKILL.md), naming remaining gaps that affect use.

Done when the requested deliverables exist and their material checks are complete or explicitly limited. No-fitting-playbook is a routing problem, not permission to expand scope. Propose reusable guidance only after the work demonstrates a recurring need.
