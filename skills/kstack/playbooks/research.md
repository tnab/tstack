# Research

Deliver a supported answer or the requested source.

1. Use [search](../../search/SKILL.md) with the question, scope, and time window. A lookup may need one source; a decision's history may need several.
2. If the user needs a synthesis of a substantial record, pass the gathered evidence to [brief](../../brief/SKILL.md). If they ask what to choose, add [decide](../../decide/SKILL.md). Do not turn an explanation of a past decision into a new recommendation.
3. Verify the central claims against their sources, retain material gaps, and deliver the answer through [write](../../write/SKILL.md).

Done when the question is answered with retrievable evidence, or the unresolved part and missing evidence are explicit. Finding follow-ups does not authorize creating tasks.
