# Review

Deliver consequential findings grounded in the artifact and its evidence.

1. Use [review](../../review/SKILL.md) with the artifact, its purpose, and available sources. Keep a review read-only unless the user also requests edits.
2. Recompute important quantities and trace disputed claims to the record. Use [search](../../search/SKILL.md) only when outside investigation is authorized and can resolve a material finding.
3. Report actionable findings with locators and reasons; do not manufacture criticism to fill a quota. For authorized edits, apply accepted corrections and inspect the revised artifact.
4. Finish the findings or revised prose through [write](../../write/SKILL.md), preserving material verification limits.

Done when the important defects or absence of consequential findings are accurately reported. A polished rewrite is not evidence that the original claims were correct.
