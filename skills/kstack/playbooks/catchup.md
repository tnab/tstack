# Catch-up

Deliver current state and the meaningful changes since a baseline.

1. Give [catchup](../../catchup/SKILL.md) the topic, baseline, as-of cutoff, and permitted sources. Without a baseline, describe the result as a current-state brief rather than implying a complete account of changes.
2. Use [search](../../search/SKILL.md) for material gaps within scope. Distinguish verified current state, last-known state, and proposals. Do not use later records to answer a historical cutoff.
3. Add [followups](../../followups/SKILL.md) when the requested result includes reconciling open actions. Keep unsupported suggested next steps separate from commitments.
4. Verify the most consequential changes and finish through [write](../../write/SKILL.md).

Done when the reader knows what changed, what stands now, and which unresolved matters need attention. Do not search unrelated chat history to reconstruct context.
