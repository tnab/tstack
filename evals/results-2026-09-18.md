# Initial behavioral results

Version tested: `0.2.0-alpha.1`. Date: 2026-09-18.

Three fresh evaluator agents ran the seven requests in [cases.json](cases.json) using only the relevant synthetic fixtures and skill guidance. Expected checks were withheld. Related requests shared an evaluator session. The outputs were inspected against the checks; they were not graded by exact wording.

| Case | Observed result |
|---|---|
| Brief | Passed. Preserved the conditional October 1 date, explained the original rationale, and excluded the later decision. The answer was 164 words. |
| Review | Passed. Found the false approval and clearance claims, incorrect checklist obligations, percentage errors, unsupported generalization, and unsupported causality. The memo remained unchanged. |
| Meeting notes | Passed. Used only the meeting transcript, preserved objections and conditional commitments, and did not invent agreement or later completion. |
| Meeting preparation | Passed. Produced a 20-minute agenda with the decision owner, alternatives, and missing evidence. No scheduling or sending occurred. |
| Follow-ups | Passed. Completed O-1, corrected the erroneous O-2 assignment with its history retained, kept O-3 blocked and O-4 open, and invented no deadlines. A repeated reconciliation preserved all four IDs and added no rows. |
| Decide | Passed. Recognized that no option met all hard constraints, recommended no immediate selection, and identified a concrete feasibility check without inventing costs or approval. |
| Catch-up | Passed. Used the requested baseline and cutoff, distinguished proposed from approved changes, and separated a suggested next step from recorded commitments. |

Structural checks passed for all four manifests, all eight skill headers, local Markdown links, and evaluation references. The bundled skill-creator checks also accepted each skill.

This is one synthetic run per scenario, plus one repeated reconciliation. There was no comparison against a baseline or independent client-installation test. These results do not establish live connector behavior, consistent performance across models, or reliability on arbitrary records.
